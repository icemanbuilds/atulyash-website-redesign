# Atulyash redesign repo

Design 2B (`blends/finalists/2b/`) is the locked main design. Its look and principles override every other design in this repo. Build every new page or element in the 2B language:
- the fixed wheat wall (`blends/glass.css`), always visible;
- rounded floating panels alternating yellow #FFCF39 and warm white #FFFAF0, with wide gaps so the wheat shows;
- abstract corner sweeps and blob-shaped photos, no scallops, arches or retro ornament;
- a white pill masthead;
- Georgia bold headings with an italic second line, system sans body at 16 px;
- green #365E12 pill buttons and 44 px tap targets;
- phone first at 390 px.

Never use the em dash. No footer text strips. Plain copy, real facts only, delivery area "Delhi NCR".
